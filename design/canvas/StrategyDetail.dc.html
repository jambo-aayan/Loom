Low-Vol Compounder
investment
Looks for large-cap stocks and index ETFs whose price has been unusually calm lately — quieter than most of their peers — on the idea that low-volatility names tend to drift upward steadily rather than swing wildly. Buys into that calm, holds for a modest, pre-set gain, and gets out quickly if it's wrong. Aims for frequent small wins over large risky moves — this is the strategy built to feel like "quick, confirmed wins."
Parameters · v3
20 days
Volatility window
How much price history feeds the low-volatility filter. Shorter reacts faster to changing conditions; longer is more stable but slower to notice a name has gotten choppier.
bottom 30%
Volatility threshold
Only considers instruments calmer than 70% of their peers. Lower = a stricter, smaller universe of rarer but calmer candidates.
+3.0%
Profit target
Take-profit level from entry price. This is arithmetic, not a forecast — hitting it is a near-certain, high-confidence exit.
−2.5%
Loss limit
Stop-loss level from entry price. Tightened in v3 (was −3.5%) after backtesting showed it improved drawdown without hurting win rate.
daily
Rebalance check
How often the strategy re-evaluates its universe for new candidates and checks existing positions against their exit plan.
